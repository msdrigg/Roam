# Roam

A free Roku remote for your Apple devices, with no ads.

<img src="./docs/static/img/roam-icon.png" alt="Roam app icon" width="240">

## Features

*   Runs on Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Vision Pro
*   Keyboard shortcuts on Mac, plus keyboard and volume button control on iPhone and iPad
*   Control your TV with Shortcuts, widgets or Siri without opening the app
*   Private listening on Mac, iPhone, iPad and Vision Pro (play the audio from your TV through your headphones)
*   Finds Roku devices on your local network as soon as you open the app
*   Built with Apple's native SwiftUI design system
*   Small download (8 MB on iPhone, 6 MB on Mac) and opens instantly

![Roam on iPhone and Mac](./docs/static/img/screenshots.png)

## How to Use

*   Open `Roam.xcodeproj` in Xcode 26 or later
*   Run on iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS or visionOS

**_OR_**

*   Download it on the App Store [here](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/roam/6469834197)
