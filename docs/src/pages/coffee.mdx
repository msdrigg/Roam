## Buy me a coffee

Roam is free, ad-free, and open source. There is no paid tier, no subscription, and
nothing is held back from you. I build it in my spare time because I wanted a Roku
remote that put its users first.

If Roam has been useful to you and you feel like chipping in, you can leave a tip below.
It is entirely optional and it changes nothing about the app — everything stays free
either way.

Payments are handled securely by [Stripe](https://stripe.com). I never see your card
details.

### A few notes

-   **This is a tip, not a donation.** I am an individual developer, not a registered
    charity, so tips are not tax-deductible.
-   **Nothing is unlocked by tipping here.** Tips on this page are purely a thank-you.
    If you would like the cosmetic extras — custom accent colors and the alternate app
    icons — those are available as a tip inside the app itself, on the App Store.
-   **Refunds.** If you tip by mistake, email
    [roam-support@msd3.io](mailto:roam-support@msd3.io) and I will refund it, no
    questions asked.

Thank you for using Roam. Genuinely — a note saying the app is useful to you is worth
as much to me as the coffee.
